Flagship Service

Rig Operations Management on Behalf of Owners

Full operational management of MODUs on behalf of their owners (manning, maintenance, drilling operations, HSE, class and regulatory compliance and performance), delivered with Tier-1 governance. Sonat Offshore acts as the owner’s operating arm, combining frontline offshore leadership with rigorous management systems, controls and reporting. This is the foundation of our path to owning and operating our own fleet.

Contact SONAT

Our Approach

  • Embed competent operations leadership and crewing aligned to owner objectives.
  • Run HSE, class and regulatory compliance as one disciplined management system.
  • Protect uptime through planned maintenance and integrity discipline.
  • Report transparently against clear KPIs and drive continuous improvement.

Rig Inspection and Technical Surveys

Independent condition assessments, acceptance and pre-purchase inspections, and SPS / class-recertification readiness surveys across drillships, semis and jack-ups. We give owners, investors and operators a clear, evidence-based view of asset condition, risk and the work required to reach a compliant, operable state.

Contact SONAT

Our Approach

  • Scope inspections against DS-1, API and class society standards.
  • Deploy surveyors with real rig, shipyard and OEM experience.
  • Deliver ranked findings with clear remediation roadmaps and cost drivers.
  • Support verification and close-out of corrective actions.

Project Management

Project leadership for rig upgrades, newbuild commissioning, reactivations, and capital projects, with rigorous schedule control, risk management, and stakeholder alignment. We take projects from initiation through close-out with the planning, controls and interface discipline that protect schedule, budget and quality.

Contact SONAT

Our Approach

  • Front-end planning, work breakdown and integrated, resource-loaded schedules.
  • Earned-value cost and schedule control: CPI, SPI and EAC.
  • Proactive risk and change management with a live register.
  • Disciplined yard, class and stakeholder interface management.

Asset Management and Maintenance

Lifecycle asset management for MODU, covering PMS/CMMS optimization, integrity management, regulatory compliance, and strategic planning to maximize uptime and long-term asset value. We turn maintenance and integrity data into decisions that extend asset life and protect economics.

Contact SONAT

Our Approach

  • Build and optimise PMS/CMMS, aligned to ISO 14224 taxonomy.
  • Apply criticality-based maintenance and spares strategies.
  • Manage integrity, certification and regulatory compliance.
  • Use data-driven planning to maximise uptime and long-term value.
