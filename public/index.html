What does SONAT Offshore do?

SONAT helps drilling rig owners and operators manage offshore assets. Its work covers rig operations, capital projects, asset integrity, inspections and maintenance. Its digital platforms help teams track assets and projects in one place.

Which types of rigs does SONAT support?

SONAT supports drillships, semisubmersibles and jack-up rigs across their operating lifecycle.

Can SONAT manage a rig on behalf of its owner?

Yes. SONAT can manage people, maintenance, HSE, compliance and performance on behalf of a rig owner.

Does SONAT support rig reactivation and commissioning?

Yes. SONAT plans and delivers reactivations, upgrades, surveys, new-build commissioning and other complex capital work.

What inspection and asset integrity services do you offer?

We assess asset condition and readiness, support certification and class requirements, and plan integrity and maintenance work across the rig lifecycle.

What are AssetOS and ProjectOS?

AssetOS brings equipment, integrity and certification records together. ProjectOS helps teams control scope, schedule, cost, risk and changes.

How do we start a project with SONAT?

Contact SONAT with your asset, scope and objectives. The team will review the requirements and agree the practical next steps with you.
