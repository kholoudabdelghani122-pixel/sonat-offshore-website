Rig Manager · MODU

Complete your details and upload your CV and certificates. Fields marked * are required.

Job Brief:

Lead the safe, compliant and high-performance operation of a mobile offshore drilling unit on behalf of its owner.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Own day-to-day operational, HSE and class compliance performance of the unit.
  • Lead the offshore management team and contractor interfaces.
  • Drive maintenance, integrity and uptime against KPIs.
  • Manage budgets, reporting and owner communication.

Requirements:

  • 15+ years offshore, with senior MODU operations leadership.
  • Strong HSE, class and regulatory knowledge.
  • Experience across drillships, semis or jack-ups.
  • IWCF/IADC and relevant certifications.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience in [industry/sector, e.g., technology, real estate, healthcare]
  • Demonstrated ability to communicate complex financial concepts effectively
